What this is

mc-analytics is an internal tool of Maincast. It collects statistics of social media accounts that belong to Maincast — live viewers, views, reactions, comments, follower counts — and shows them on one dashboard for the team.

Who can use it

Maincast staff with an account issued by us. There is no public sign-up, the tool is not offered as a service to anyone else, and access can be withdrawn at any time.

What it does with a connected account

It reads statistics, and that is all. It does not post, comment, reply, send messages, follow, like, or change anything on a connected account.

No guarantees

The tool is provided as is. We do not promise that it works, that it is available, or that its numbers are complete or correct. All figures come from the platforms themselves and can be delayed, rounded, missing or simply wrong. Nothing shown here is advice of any kind, and we are not responsible for decisions made on the basis of these numbers, to the extent the law allows.

Ending access

Whoever owns a connected account can revoke our access at any time in that platform's own settings. When that happens we stop collecting and delete the stored access token. We can also stop collecting from any account at any time, without notice.

The platforms

Data from each platform is also subject to that platform's own terms, and those terms come first. TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, Twitch, Telegram and X belong to their respective owners; we are not affiliated with them, and they do not sponsor or endorse this tool.
