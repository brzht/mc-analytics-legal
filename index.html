mc-analytics

Internal analytics tool of Maincast.

Maincast runs esports and sports broadcasts and publishes them across several social platforms. mc-analytics collects statistics of Maincast's own channels — live viewers, views of videos and posts, reactions, comments and follower counts — stores them as a long time series and shows them on one internal dashboard.

The platforms themselves keep only a short history: a live viewer count exists only while the stream is running, and stream archives disappear after weeks. The tool records those numbers as they happen so the team can see how a channel changes over months.

What it does with a connected account

  • Reads statistics of that account on a schedule.
  • Nothing else: it never posts, comments, messages, follows or changes anything.

Who uses it

Maincast staff only. There is no public sign-up, and the data is not resold or shared.
