Privacy Policy

mc-analytics · last updated 24 September 2026

In one line

We collect statistics about our own channels. We do not sell data, we do not advertise with it, and we do not gather information about private individuals.

What we collect

  • Statistics of Maincast's own accounts: the list of videos, streams and posts with their titles, links, covers, publication times and durations, and the counters attached to them — views, live viewers, likes and other reactions, comments, shares, follower and subscriber counts. This is either data that is public on the platform or data the account owner authorised us to read.
  • Public counters of other public professional accounts — for example how many views a publicly posted video of another broadcaster has — when we compare our channels with the rest of the market.
  • Access tokens for accounts that were connected with their owner's permission.
  • Staff sign-in data: the name, email address and password hash of the Maincast employees who log into the dashboard.

What we do not collect

We do not collect data about viewers, followers or commenters as people. We do not read direct messages. We do not store lists of who follows or who commented, we do not build profiles of individuals, and we do not track anyone across sites.

Why we collect it

To see how our own channels perform over time. The platforms keep only a short history of these numbers — a live viewer count exists only while the stream runs — so the tool records them as they happen.

Where it is kept

On a server rented by us in the European Union, and with the hosting providers that serve the dashboard. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Only Maincast staff can reach the data.

How long

Statistics are kept for as long as the tool exists — a long history is the whole point of it. Access tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected or the token is revoked.

Who else sees it

Nobody outside Maincast. We do not sell the data, we do not pass it to advertisers or data brokers, and the dashboard carries no third-party analytics or tracking. We would disclose data only if the law required it.

Your choices

If you authorised us to read one of your accounts, you can revoke that access at any time in the platform's own settings — on TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions, on Meta platforms under Business settings or Apps and websites. After that we stop collecting from the account and delete its token.

To ask what we hold, to correct it or to have it deleted, write to info@maincast.com.
